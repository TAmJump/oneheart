Where this stands

The circle is being built now. The monthly call starts once the first group of holders is in, and the gatherings follow the map — they happen where holders are, so the places are set by who joins. Dates are announced to holders first.

Anything on this page that is not yet running is described as a plan, not a promise. What is fixed is the record: places stay open until 30 June 2027, the artworks are made as they stand on that date, and certificates are delivered digitally by 30 September 2027.

What the circle is not

Membership carries no financial return, no ownership share, no revenue interest and no guaranteed resale value. It is a room, not an asset. This is set out in full in the participation terms.
