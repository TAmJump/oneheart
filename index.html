A participatory mosaic art project. Send one portrait photograph and it becomes one piece of an artwork built from thousands of others. Your face stays in the place it is given, and an artwork is finished only when every place in it is filled.

22 faces, and one that holds them all

Twenty-two artworks carry a face each — identity, wonder, love, courage, loss, hope, good and evil. The twenty-third is not another character. It is where the other twenty-two come together, and it is built from 5,000 portraits instead of 2,500. Read in order, the twenty-three trace one life.

Two sides, one story

Every piece has a reverse. The front carries your face; the back carries your name or initials. The reverse sides of all twenty-three artworks are built from names — and when they are turned over and brought together, they form another image entirely.
