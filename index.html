Why these 22 characters exist

The ideas are not mine.
I only drew them.

Between 2024 and 2025 I met children living with illness. The characters came out of their ideas, and I drew them. Twenty-two became the foundation of ONE HEART.

You don’t have to work so hard for me. Said by a child who already understood what the people around him were carrying. Most of them are treated with money their parents earned, and with support from public systems. They know it, and they carry it.

So I started from the other side. If what came from them carried its own value — as artwork, then as products — the value could come back to them. They would no longer only be the ones receiving.

If the characters become products, the goal is for the value to return to the children whose ideas they came from. And if it works, it should not stop at twenty-two.

How one artwork is made

One photo becomes one piece.

Choose an artwork. Back it with ¥500. Send one portrait photograph. Your photo is placed as a single piece and stays in that position. When all 2,500 places are filled, the artwork is completed and delivered to everyone inside it.

Nothing is generated to fill a gap. Every piece is a real person.

Every piece is a person. Every person is a beat.

The hidden second side

Two sides. One story.

Every piece has a reverse. The front carries your face; the back carries your name or initials. The reverse sides of all twenty-three artworks are built from names — and when they are turned over and brought together, they form another image entirely.

That image is the reason this project is called ONE HEART.

Front: your portrait. Back: your name.
60,000 names, assembled on the reverse side.

What you receive

Your certificate, and your ID.

You receive one certificate for every piece you hold. It shows the artwork number and title, your piece number and its exact position, the date of completion, and a certificate ID with QR verification.

Every certificate you hold is linked to a single member ID — one per person. It is how ONE HEART recognises you as the project continues. Everything is delivered digitally; nothing is shipped.
