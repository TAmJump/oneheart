Why participation continues.

One artwork holds 2,500 places. Twenty-three artworks hold 60,000. A single thirty-day campaign cannot reach that, and it is not meant to. Places stay open here until 30 June 2027, and on that date every artwork is produced with the places taken by then.

Kickstarter can only take pledges while the campaign is running. So the door for everyone who arrives later is here. An artwork is never cancelled for being short of people — a place still open is carried by the artwork's own colour, and the artwork is finished and delivered either way.
