YOU ARE THE CEO.
Starting today, you run this.

NOT JUST SOMEONE WHO RECEIVES HELP.
CREATE VALUE. CHOOSE YOUR FUTURE.

The characters began as ideas from children living with illness, whom I met between 2024 and 2025. I drew them, and twenty-two of those drawings became the artworks of ONE HEART. In this project the child who had the idea is not treated as someone being helped. They are treated as the creator, and as the person in charge.

01 / WHY THIS SYSTEM EXISTS

“YOU DON'T HAVE TO WORK SO HARD FOR ME.”

A child living with illness is carried by the effort of their family, the care of medical staff, and the public systems built around them. Some of those children understand exactly how much the adults around them are giving up, and quietly wish that nobody would have to push themselves that hard on their behalf.

THEN LET'S BUILD A FUTURE YOU CAN CHOOSE WITH THE VALUE YOU CREATE.

What ONE HEART wants to change

  • Not fixing anyone in the role of the one who receives.
  • Turning a child's imagination into artwork, products and intellectual property.
  • Building a route by which the value they created reaches their own future.
  • Taking that model to the rest of the world.

05 / FOR YOUR FAMILY

NOW MAKE YOUR FAMILY SMILE.

The profit does not have to go to treatment alone. It can buy a present for the family who carried you this far. It can pay for a trip together, or a meal somewhere you have always wanted to eat.

“TODAY, IT'S ON ME.”

From support to circulation

Instead of one side giving and the other side receiving, the child creates value, that value returns to the child and their family, and what it leaves behind opens the door for the next child. That circle is what ONE HEART is built for.

07 / A GLOBAL STANDARD

MAKE THIS A GLOBAL STANDARD.

A child living with illness should not only be waiting for support. Their imagination can become an asset, and that asset can become a choice.

Products and licensing

CHOICE
Treatment, life, future
Taking this model to other countries requires the protection of minors' rights, guardian consent, copyright and trademark, revenue sharing, taxation, and rules on how funds may be applied to medical costs — each designed country by country, in law and in practice. Becoming a global standard is the goal we are working towards. It is not a claim that such a system already exists.
